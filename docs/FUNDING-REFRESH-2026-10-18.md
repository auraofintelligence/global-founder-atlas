## What verification means

Every record contains a source-check date, URLs, confirmed fields and unverified fields. Reaching a homepage does not verify a live application, award agreement, Australian eligibility, IP or co-funding. Unverified conditions are labelled in the rendered detail. Scores remain assessments under the existing weights, not eligibility decisions.

## Archive and maintenance convention

`data/opportunity-archive.json` holds full pre-refresh snapshots with unique round IDs, programme IDs, official sources, closing dates, reasons and archive dates. Never delete a recurring programme merely because a round ends. Keep past snapshots unchanged when a new round opens. A programme closure without a published date is recorded without inventing a dated round.

`data/funding-refresh.json` records the run date, scope and counts. Update it with reviewed opportunity records and a dated audit. The project portfolio and proposed keyword mapping are outside this refresh.
